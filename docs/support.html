Frequently asked questions

How do I share a live scoreboard?

Open a session's scoreboard and tap the broadcast icon. The app generates a short code (format XXXX-XXXX) and a QR code. Anyone with the code or link can follow the scoreboard live in their browser or in the app — no account needed.

How do I stop sharing a live session?

Delete the session from history, or simply finish the session. Inactive sessions older than 30 days are automatically removed from the server.

Is my data backed up?

Gameplay data is stored locally on your device and is not backed up to any cloud service. Uninstalling the app deletes all session history.

How do I delete my data?

Inside the app: Settings → Data → Delete all history. For details, see our data deletion page.

Why am I seeing ads?

ScoreWise is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. On iOS you can decline tracking and ads will be non-personalized. See our privacy policy for details.

Something looks wrong or the app crashed

Email us with what you were doing when it happened, your device model, OS version, and app version. Screenshots help a lot.
